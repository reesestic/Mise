# Profile

## Purpose

Give the user one place to set the personal context that shapes recommendations and planning throughout Mise.

## User value

Let Mise become more relevant over time without forcing users to restate their goals, tastes, needs, or shopping defaults on every screen.

## What it does

- Store dietary needs and restrictions, nutrition goals, taste preferences, disliked foods, and favorite cuisines or ingredients.
- Store cooking context such as available time, effort, skill, and appliances/equipment owned where useful.
- Let users express practical cooking preferences such as quick meals, fewer dishes/low cleanup, and making enough for leftovers. These guide ranking, not a mandatory meal schedule.
- Store shopping preferences such as budget defaults, household size, and preferred units.
- Provide account and app settings.
- Make clear which preferences are strict restrictions and which are flexible goals.

## What it does not do

- It is not a primary destination or a fourth daily task tab in the current direction.
- It should not require extensive setup before users can explore meals or manage groceries.
- It should not silently override a user's explicit choice on another screen.

## Connections to other areas

- **Profile → Cook Now:** Personalizes meal ranking, filters, and explanations.
- **Profile → Groceries:** Shapes meal suggestions, ingredient recommendations, budget defaults, and tradeoffs.
- **Profile → Pantry:** May inform units or household context, but does not alter inventory without user action.
- **Profile equipment → Cook Now:** Filters or ranking can avoid meals requiring appliances the user does not have.

## AI role

Use preferences as context and explain relevant matches. Ask for clarification when a stated preference conflicts with a strict restriction or request; allow the user to revise their profile.

## Aesthetic direction

Organized, concise, and easy to revisit. Group preferences by food, nutrition, cooking, shopping, and account. Keep access available from the primary screens without competing with their main tasks.

## Open questions

- Which profile fields are essential to start, and which can be learned progressively?
- How should strict dietary restrictions be distinguished from goals and likes?
- Should budget be a default, a per-shop request, or both?
