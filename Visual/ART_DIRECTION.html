Name exploration

Mise has room to grow.

The core idea is a connected food loop: shop with meals in mind, keep track of what you own, and cook what fits today. “Grocerease” is friendly, but can sound like grocery convenience alone.

MiseGood BasketPantry PotNext MealGather & CookMeal GardenGrocerease

Working recommendation: keep Mise as the lead and explain it with “Shop for meals, make the most of what you have.” Other lines to explore: “Shop with your next meal in mind.” · “Make good things from what you have.” Naming and visual cues are original explorations inspired by broad toy-like warmth, not copies of the attached references. Check name availability before committing.
